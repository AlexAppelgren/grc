What it is

Sets the detailed conduct and organisation rules for firms that run a securities business in Sweden. It covers how clients are classified and informed, how advice is tested for suitability, how orders and costs are recorded, and how client assets are kept apart. Finansinspektionen issued it to carry the detailed MiFID II rules into Swedish supervision.
