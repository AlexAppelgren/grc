Members card. Role pills are information pills labelled from the role rows; "Awaiting enrolment" is a warning pill from the invited kind, "Deactivated" an information pill. Re-issue enrolment, role changes and deactivation ask for a passkey (auth-step-up.html); "last admin" answers the server's 409 in place. Another tenant's member URL renders "Not found", never Restricted. Two buttons share one row on phones, primary on the right. Teams (TEN-03, chunk 8) are shown on every row and set here, because team membership is members.manage while the teams themselves are vocab.manage and live under Organisation. Saving teams writes one audit event holding the team keys before and after, and a member of another tenant is refused the same way an unknown id is. Changing teams grants nothing and needs no step-up. Deactivating a member also ends their participations and their team memberships. The removal preview (TEN-05, chunk 8) opens from Deactivate and reads GET /tenant/members/{id}/open-work: one line per kind the member owns, with its count, and a new owner for each, a person or a team, picked from the people and team rows. It then says how many items they take part in and which teams they leave, because both simply end, and that nothing changes until the admin confirms. Confirming sends the owner per kind on DELETE /tenant/members/{id} behind a passkey, and the server moves every item and deactivates the member in one transaction, one audit event per item. Sending no owner for a kind with open work answers 422 reassignment_required with the counts, rendered in the dialog from the code; an owner who is not an active member, or is the member being removed, answers 422 unknown_member. What a team owns never needs a new owner. The kinds drawn are chunk 8's (obligations, obligations per legal entity, gaps, recurring duty dates, internal items) with chunk 9's cases and actions; the kind names are labels from the catalog, the counts are the server's.
