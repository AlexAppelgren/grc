My access tokens card (/me/tokens), in the account menu beside My passkeys and My sessions. The list is the member's own tokens: prefix, what it reads as (me, or me within a named agent), its scopes as information pills, created, expires and last use, with Expired (warning) and Revoked (information) from the row's facts; no kind pill, since every row here is personal. Create needs tokens create and a passkey: a name, at least one of the four read scopes, where a scope the member's own permissions do not back is disabled with its reason, an optional agent access entry, and a required expiry of at most 90 days. The plain token shows once. Revoke asks once and needs no passkey. Without tokens create there is no Create button and a sentence says why; tokens already held stay listed and revocable. An administrator sees and revokes every token in API keys (admin-api-keys.html). Two buttons per row on a phone, the primary on the right.
