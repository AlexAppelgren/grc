Vocabulary card. Tone is never chosen here: a tenant tag is always outlined information, a flag always brand, a sub-status takes its category's tone. The admin supplies a label, translations and a usage note, nothing else. A system value can be relabelled but not retired. Rename is inline and keeps the key; reorder stores sort_order; retire shows the usage count first; merge previews the count moved and writes one audit entry. "Did you mean Custody?" answers the server's 409 near_duplicate in place. Keyboard: the grip accepts arrow up and down. Two buttons per row on phones, primary on the right.
