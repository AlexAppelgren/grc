Shell card. Sizes follow design/system/foundations.md: 32px rows, 16px icons, 14px labels, a 68px wordmark (64px in the phone header). The rail is a neutral surface with a hairline, not a block of brand green, in both themes. The current row has a small radius, a neutral tint and medium weight, never a full pill. Every destination the registry unlocks is listed, grouped as the registry groups them, separated by space. "Minimise menu" or ctrl/cmd+b collapses the rail to icons (the wordmark becomes the open e alone, each row gets a tooltip) and the choice is remembered on this device. On phones the dock is gone: the menu button opens the same rail as a sheet, so Roadmap and Admin are reachable there too. A count, once a screen feeds one, is a quiet muted number at the end of its row. The prototype's "Switch user" never ships. The wordmark here is a stand-in for design/brand/logo-phonetic.svg.
