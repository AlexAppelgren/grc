Shell card. Sizes follow design/system/foundations.md: 32px rows, 16px icons, 14px labels, a 68px wordmark (64px in the compact header). The rail is a neutral surface with a hairline, not a block of brand green, in both themes. The current row has a small radius, a neutral tint and medium weight, never a full pill. Every destination the registry unlocks is listed, grouped as the registry groups them, separated by space. "Minimise menu" or ctrl/cmd+b collapses the rail to icons (the wordmark becomes the open e alone, each row gets a tooltip) and the choice is remembered on this device. The rail shows from 1024px. Below that, design/system/navigation.md applies: a floating tab bar takes the rail's place, with the registry's dock destinations (Today, Watch, Inventory and Search), then More. The bar is 64px tall with cells of at most 96px, a 12px radius, a hairline and the float shadow. It floats 16px above the bottom edge and from the sides, clear of any safe area. A tab is a 20px icon over a label that wraps rather than truncates. The current tab has the neutral tint, medium weight and a 1px outline, never a full pill. While the current page lives in More, More takes that treatment. More opens the sheet drawn above: Roadmap and Admin in the rail's groups, then the account laid flat (name, organisation and role, My passkeys, My sessions, Sign out). The sheet is at most 560px wide and covers the bar. A choice, Close, Escape, the scrim, a route change or a width of 1024px closes it. The wordmark stays as a line at the top of the page and scrolls away. A count, once a screen feeds one, is a quiet muted number at the end of its row. The prototype's "Switch user" never ships. The wordmark here is a stand-in for design/brand/logo-phonetic.svg.
