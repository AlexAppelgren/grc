Support access card (/admin/support-access), under Admin beside Security. It follows D-49 and ADR 0042. A platform person asks from the console with a purpose, an optional ticket and up to SUPPORT_ACCESS_MAX_HOURS (4); the request grants nothing, emails the members who hold security manage, and lapses after SUPPORT_ACCESS_REQUEST_TTL_HOURS (24) if nobody decides. Approve needs security manage and a passkey, and the window starts at approval; Decline and Revoke need security manage and no passkey, because they only take access away. The approver is never the person asking, which the database enforces. The state is a pill from the grant's own kind: "Waiting for approval" is warning, as everywhere it appears; Active is notice, like a running agent; Ended, Revoked, Declined and Lapsed are information. The platform person is named here, with their role, because the bank must know who reads its data; the console never names a member of the bank. The history lists each support access read row under its grant, with the route template and the path ids and never a query or a body, and links to the audit log filtered to that grant. The banner and the two refusals are drawn once here and used on every tenant screen a support session opens. Names, tickets and times are sample data.
