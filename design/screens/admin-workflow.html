Workflow card (COL-02 and TEN-01; chunk 10). Route /admin/workflow, an Admin destination of its own, gated by workflow manage (CHUNK10_TASKS.md ruling 8: it is not a section of admin-organisation.html, which members manage gates). It reads the "workflow" object of GET /tenant and saves the whole object with PATCH /tenant/workflow. No step-up: this is a workflow control, not a security one. No four eyes and no request: a save applies at once and writes one audit event with before and after.

Six fields, each with its unit beside the number and the platform default in its hint, so a bank sees what it is changing from. Reminder lead days are two small lists of day counts (before a due date, before a review), each 1 to 90, at most 5, no repeats, sorted from the largest down on save. The last chip in a list has no remove button: the server refuses an empty list, and a person who wants no reminders turns them off for themselves on /notifications. The escalation threshold is 1 to 90 days overdue. The escalation role lists the organisation's roles by label, sent as the role's key; the hint says who else is told, because escalation reaches the head of the owner's department too. The digest weekday lists the seven days by label, sent as a weekday key; the hint names the organisation's timezone from GET /tenant. The triage target is 1 to 720 hours.

"Reset to platform defaults" fills the form with the defaults from the same response and saves nothing until Save. Client checks mirror the server's; the server's 422 names the field and renders under it, 422 unknown_key under the role or the weekday, a 403 in place with the permission it needs. A member without workflow manage never sees the destination; opening the address directly shows the shared Restricted screen (states.html).

No pills on this page. Values, the role list and the timezone are sample data; each default is a setting with an environment override.
