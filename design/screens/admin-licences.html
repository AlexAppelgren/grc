Licences card (TEN-02, TEN-07, ADM-01; TEN-S16; Alex 2026-11-12). Any member reads the page, which lists every legal entity of the bank in the Organisation page's order, one card each; people are not tied to single companies yet. The head row names the company, its legal-entity term as the scope block's brand pill, its organisation number and country, and, for someone holding register.read and an active company, links to What applies for that company; an inactive company is dimmed and marked Inactive, as on the Organisation page. From the register shows only for a company read from the public registers: the authority, the date read and the source (a link only when the address is an absolute http or https address), then the main business, the other businesses and each licence line as the register words it, with the grant date. Under each line, the scope terms bleqq's mapping reads from it as brand pills under Brings into scope, or Not used for scope yet for a line the mapping does not know; a line the mapping knows to add nothing shows no second row. Lines are matched to their terms by their own text. After five licence lines the rest sit in a fold. Recorded by us is what the bank typed, moved from the Organisation page unchanged, with Add beside its heading and None when nothing is recorded: a licence with its type, reference, grant date, services and scope note, a certificate with its issuer, number, scope statement, dates and owner and no term; withdrawn rows behind Show withdrawn; Add and Edit for vocab.manage, no step-up. Outside this company's scope lists the exclusions an approved regulatory scope request gave it, with the way to Regulatory scope for someone who may open it. The last line, only with register.read, is how many obligations the register offers within the company's licences, linking to What applies. Register wording, names and numbers are data, never strings.
