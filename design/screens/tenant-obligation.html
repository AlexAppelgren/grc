Machine translation from Swedish. The original is authoritative.Research from third parties may be received only if it is paid from the institution's own resources, from a research payment account, or jointly with execution under the conditions set out in the rules. The institution sets criteria for an annual assessment of the quality, usability and value of the research it uses. If the research does not contribute to better investment decisions, the institution takes corrective action.

Obligation card. Header slots: instrument (brand), regime (information), binding level (information "Binding" or warning "Guidance, comply or explain"), then compliance status: the worst of the legal entities it applies to, with the entity named beside it. The legal text block shows one version in one language; "Show what changed" marks sentences added and removed against the previous version; "As of" picks the version in force on that date. Translations are rows: the original carries no label, a machine translation carries the label above the text. Scope terms are brand pills, "All services" when every term is selected, "Not client-specific" when the list is empty. Library tags follow as brand pills, tenant tags outlined. Nothing here edits the library: re-verification and corrections happen in the console; a member reports. Reported problems (AUD-03, D-50) sits under provenance on the obligation and the instrument card: the reports this bank filed on the record, newest first, each with its reporter, time, words, the version and language on screen, and a status pill by kind (open warning, fixed positive, answered and rejected information). A colleague holding proposals.create reads and closes every one; anyone else reads and closes their own. Closing takes an outcome and a required note, needs no step-up, and never changes the library; a report closed by someone else first answers in place. Nobody outside the bank, bleqq included, sees a report, and the console has no surface for one. Participants (COL-04, chunk 8) hang on the register entry, which is created on its first write, not on the library record: the owners sit read-only above the list, each participant names who added them, adding needs register edit and anyone may leave their own row. A person or a team is one picker over the people reference and the team list; a team reaches its active members. Participation lists and notifies and grants nothing, so it needs no step-up and no second person; the server's refusals (already a participant, cannot open the record, the participant cap, the missing permission) render in place. The register panels (chunk 8) are the bank's own judgement over the library record. Does it apply to us holds one row per legal entity the obligation spans, each with its pill, reason, who and when; a holder of applicability approve answers, then confirms in a dialog, and the answer is stored at once with one audit event, no second person and no passkey. A member without it reads. Where we stand holds, per legal entity, the compliance pill, status note, risk, first-line owner (a person or a team), compliance contact and next review; the header shows the worst entity and names it. A change asks how it was assessed, so the history explains itself, and a stale write answers in place with Reload. Does not apply hides no fact: the last status and its gaps stay on record and return if it applies again. Gaps lists the obligation's gaps with their status, severity and owner and opens Record a gap. Linked internal items picks an item the bank has or creates one, and removing a link keeps the item. How we read this rule is a version with its author and date, with no approval step, and the assessment history sits beneath it, append-only. On a standard, the Units panel lists the following entities and opens the units page. Every string goes to the register catalogs in en and sv.
