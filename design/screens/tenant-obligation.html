Third-party payments
Machine translation from Swedish. The original is authoritative.Research from third parties may be received only if it is paid from the institution's own resources, from a research payment account, or jointly with execution under the conditions set out in the rules. The institution sets criteria for an annual assessment of the quality, usability and value of the research it uses. If the research does not contribute to better investment decisions, the institution takes corrective action.

Obligation card. Header slots: instrument (brand), regime (information), binding level (information "Binding" or warning "Guidance, comply or explain"), then compliance status once chunk 8 lands. The legal text block shows one version in one language; "Show what changed" marks sentences added and removed against the previous version; "As of" picks the version in force on that date. Translations are rows: the original carries no label, a machine translation carries the label above the text. Scope terms are brand pills, "All services" when every term is selected, "Not client-specific" when the list is empty. Library tags follow as brand pills, tenant tags outlined. Nothing here edits the library: re-verification and corrections happen in the console; a member reports. Reported problems (AUD-03, D-50) sits under provenance on the obligation and the instrument card: the reports this bank filed on the record, newest first, each with its reporter, time, words, the version and language on screen, and a status pill by kind (open warning, fixed positive, answered and rejected information). A colleague holding proposals.create reads and closes every one; anyone else reads and closes their own. Closing takes an outcome and a required note, needs no step-up, and never changes the library; a report closed by someone else first answers in place. Nobody outside the bank, bleqq included, sees a report, and the console has no surface for one. Participants (COL-04, chunk 8) hang on the register entry, which is created on its first write, not on the library record: the owners sit read-only above the list, each participant names who added them, adding needs register edit and anyone may leave their own row. A person or a team is one picker over the people reference and the team list; a team reaches its active members. Participation lists and notifies and grants nothing, so it needs no step-up and no second person; the server's refusals (already a participant, cannot open the record, the participant cap, the missing permission) render in place.
