Inventory card. One search bar and a Filters button replace the row of selects (D-103). The bar runs the hybrid search (SRC-01) over obligations and provisions, never watch changes, inside the scope and every filter that is set; what is typed stays in the component and never reaches the address bar, and Enter runs it. Clearing it returns the list. On the Instruments tab the bar finds instruments by reference or name. The sheet holds Instrument, Regime, Service, Duty type and As of on the Obligations tab, and Regime on the Instruments tab. Each value is a key; a change applies at once, so Done only closes the sheet. A set filter shows beside the button as a chip with its own Remove, followed by "Clear filters"; As of keeps its banner and "Back to today" instead of a chip. The values follow the scope: Instrument lists the instruments with obligations inside it, Regime and Service list the terms the bank's regulatory scope chose (every term when a dimension is not restricted, and under Show all items), and Duty type lists every duty type, since a duty type is a kind of duty and not part of the scope. Scope is one value of three on the tab row, because it applies to both tabs: "My scope" (the default), "Markets we watch" and "Show all items". My scope is the bank's regulatory scope narrowed to the slices of the person's teams (R2, TEN-03); until teams exist it is the bank's scope. Markets we watch lists only what watching adds, exactly as before. Show all items lifts the scope: a row outside the bank's scope renders dashed with the reason in the meta; in R2 a row inside the bank's scope but outside the person's teams shows only its applicability, never the bank's reading, compliance status, gaps or evidence, which the server withholds. The instrument pill is brand, Guidance is information from the binding kind, applicability and compliance status take their tone from the ordinal, "N open changes" is a computed notice pill, and a hit's match kind and "Provision" are information pills; the API sends facts, never a phrase. "As of" re-reads every row's version. Every role reads this screen; nothing on it writes. Instrument and obligation titles, counts and legal text here are sample data.
