Gaps card. The list reads every gap of the bank across the register, newest target first, open work by default; filters for status, severity, owner, legal entity and target date store keys and live in the address. A row carries the three header pills (status, severity, source), the title, the obligation, the legal entity when the gap names one, the owner and the target date as text, overdue in the negative text colour; a pending risk acceptance is meta text, never a fourth pill. A gap under a standard names the unit by the tenant's own reference, never a clause of the standard. The record shows what is missing, the remediation plan and the way out by status. Record a gap, Edit, Start remediation, Close and Reopen need gaps edit, confirm where they change the status, send If-Match and answer a stale write in place. Accept the risk takes a reason from the tenant's risk-acceptance list; approval needs someone else holding risk accept approve and a passkey step-up, a refusal renders from its code, and the database refuses the same person in both roles. Risk accepted reads information from its kind. Every string here goes to the register catalogs in en and sv.
