Regulatory scope card (/admin/footprint). The page is restricted to holders of footprint request or footprint approve; everyone else gets state 16, and FP-03 shows the scope to other members only where lists offer "Show outside our scope". It opens read-only: the options are checkbox glyphs with visually hidden "In our scope" or "Not in our scope", not disabled controls, so held and unheld terms stay equally readable and nothing sits in the tab order. A group with no held term says "Not restricted: every option applies.", because an empty group means no restriction (FP-01). Only dimensions that restrict and have active terms show, so channel, lifecycle stage, theme and the termless jurisdiction, licensed activity and product type are absent; jurisdiction joins as an ordinary group once it has terms. Order is by dimension, then by term. "Propose a change" is the one danger button on the page and shows only while nothing waits: it turns the groups into checkbox groups and opens the change panel, and nothing is written until "Request approval". The preview is a dry run on every keystroke of the draft, so it costs no audit row; it counts the bank's open cases beside the obligations, by the same scope rule, and a kind the server has not counted reads "not counted yet". One warn notice at most sits above the groups, and while a request waits it is the banner, not the rule. All text inside a warn or bad notice is the text colour: muted text on the dark warning fill is 3.62:1 and negative text 3.24:1, both below AA, and the change is stated in plain words rather than coloured insertion and deletion marks for the same reason. In light the warning pill and the warn banner share Green's l3-warning-02 fill, so on the banner the pill reads as its label in warning text (5.16:1, AA) rather than as a shape; on the surface, and in dark, where the pill takes the -03 step, it is a pill. The status line under the head is empty until a send, an approval, a rejection or a withdrawal puts "Sent for approval.", "Approved. The regulatory scope has changed.", "Rejected." or "Withdrawn." in it and moves focus there. A failed withdrawal, and the 409s for a request that someone else sent first or a decision that came second, render under the banner. Approval needs a second person and a fresh passkey assertion; the requester never sees Approve, and the server's refusal is state 12. Rejection needs a reason and no step-up, because it changes nothing. Two buttons share a row on a phone, the primary on the right. Counts and record titles here are sample data; the app counts the obligations a change hides and reveals under the FP-01 rule.

Markets (states 17 to 19; FP-04, AC-FP2, ADR 0026). A market is a country the platform covers, and its level is computed: operating when its jurisdiction term is in the scope, otherwise watching when a watch row exists, otherwise not followed. So the Jurisdiction group, now that the dimension mirrors the jurisdiction rows, is the markets we operate in, and it says so under its heading; it is an ordinary group, last because its dimension sorts after the prototype's seven, and it is added and removed through the same request as every other term. The panel below it writes the other level directly: one row per active country, the name and then either "Operating" as meta text or a Watching toggle that saves at once, with an audit row and no second person, because watching hides nothing. The European Union is not a country, so it has no row; what its rules reach is the "Also included" line, rendered from each jurisdiction's parent, Norway included through the EEA Agreement. "Everywhere else" says that research sweeps every market whatever is chosen here. The control is the foundations Toggle as a pressed button, not a switch, because foundations.md specifies no switch. Watching is carried by the toggle's border (border-neutral-01, 4.15:1 light and 7.05:1 dark against the card) and by the text colour, never by the fill alone, which measures 1.14:1 and would fail WCAG 1.4.11, exactly as the current tab is carried in navigation.md; aria-pressed carries it for a screen reader, and the label stays "Watching", so the accessible name holds the visible one and the market's name after it. There is no new pill and no new tone. A waiting scope request blocks the operating markets, not these toggles. A repeated watch answers 409 and an unwatched market 404; both render as state 19, and the toggle never invents a level the server did not send. Order follows the seed: Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland. The keys a market carries never reach a path or a query string (D-30), so nothing here is linkable.

Standards followed (state 20; FP-01, INV-08, AC-FP3, std-journeys). A standard is a term of the one dimension whose kind is opt_in, never a scope dimension: a record carrying a standard's term shows only to a bank whose scope names that term, and an empty group names none. So the group reads "None followed." where a scope group reads "Not restricted: every option applies.", in the read state and as the hint under its legend, and the screen tells the two apart by the dimension's kind, never its key. Following a standard reveals its records and hides nothing, so the draft never counts the group as starting to filter and the narrowing warning stays away; removing the last standard hides them again, which the preview counts and the hides warning names. It goes through the same request, second person and passkey as every other term. The group shows once its dimension has an active term; the seeded ISO/IEC 27001 term stays inactive outside E2E until the legal question in docs/TODO_FOR_alex.md is answered. New string: footprint.noneFollowed "None followed." (sv "Ingen följs.").

Scope items (states 21 to 29; OWN-01, FP-02, AC-OWN2, FP-S18, J-12; D-89, D-91, D-98, ADR 0059). A scope item names a regulation the shared library does not cover yet: a name, one jurisdiction term, one regime term, an official reference where one exists, one or more public https addresses, and an optional note for the bank's agent capped by a setting. It is a row of the regulatory scope request, not a new approval path: "Add a regulation" and Remove open the same draft as "Propose a change", so one request waits at a time, and the request carries terms, items or both. A second person holding footprint approve approves it with a passkey (state 26, then auth-step-up.html), the requester is refused with state 12 and the server's 409 four_eyes_violation, and rejection needs a reason. Approving an item widens no term, so its preview always reads "Nothing." under Hides and Reveals, and the narrowing warning never shows for it. Every route takes a person's session and a permission: no API key and no agent reaches one, and nothing on this card offers an agent a way to add, change or remove an item. What an agent does with an approved item shows here only as its research status, one pill per item by the research kind: waiting_for_agent is warning "Waiting for your agent", with the reason as meta (AI switched off, the month's budget spent, or the scope researcher not switched on under Agents, checked in that order); researching is notice "Our agent is researching", a running agent; proposals_waiting is notice "N proposals waiting", a count, with a link to our own records (tenant-private-records.html) for holders of private records approve; researched is positive "Researched", a finished run with nothing left to decide. Nobody picks a tone. The addresses are the item's own, shown as links that open outside the app with rel="noopener noreferrer", wrapping anywhere at 375 px. The form holds no rule of its own: an address that is not https or not public comes back from the server as 422 and renders under its input. Under D-98 the name, reference, note and addresses reach our own agent, capped and screened, and the hint under the note says so in plain words; nothing else of ours does. Removing an item stops the research and changes no record we already approved. The panel is part of the page, so it is denied with it (state 16), and it reads the same to someone who can only approve, without Add and Remove (state 22).
