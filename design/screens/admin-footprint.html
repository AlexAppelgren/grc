Regulatory scope card (/admin/footprint). The page is restricted to holders of footprint request or footprint approve; everyone else gets state 16, and FP-03 shows the scope to other members only where lists offer "Show outside our scope". It opens read-only: the options are checkbox glyphs with visually hidden "In our scope" or "Not in our scope", not disabled controls, so held and unheld terms stay equally readable and nothing sits in the tab order. A group with no held term says "Not restricted: every option applies.", because an empty group means no restriction (FP-01). Only dimensions that restrict and have active terms show, so channel, lifecycle stage, theme and the termless jurisdiction, licensed activity and product type are absent; jurisdiction joins as an ordinary group once it has terms. Order is by dimension, then by term. "Propose a change" is the one danger button on the page and shows only while nothing waits: it turns the groups into checkbox groups and opens the change panel, and nothing is written until "Request approval". The preview is a dry run on every keystroke of the draft, so it costs no audit row; cases read "not counted yet" until the case workflow lands. One warn notice at most sits above the groups, and while a request waits it is the banner, not the rule. All text inside a warn or bad notice is the text colour: muted text on the dark warning fill is 3.62:1 and negative text 3.24:1, both below AA, and the change is stated in plain words rather than coloured insertion and deletion marks for the same reason. In light the warning pill and the warn banner share Green's l3-warning-02 fill, so on the banner the pill reads as its label in warning text (5.16:1, AA) rather than as a shape; on the surface, and in dark, where the pill takes the -03 step, it is a pill. The status line under the head is empty until a send, an approval, a rejection or a withdrawal puts "Sent for approval.", "Approved. The regulatory scope has changed.", "Rejected." or "Withdrawn." in it and moves focus there. A failed withdrawal, and the 409s for a request that someone else sent first or a decision that came second, render under the banner. Approval needs a second person and a fresh passkey assertion; the requester never sees Approve, and the server's refusal is state 12. Rejection needs a reason and no step-up, because it changes nothing. Two buttons share a row on a phone, the primary on the right. Counts and record titles here are sample data; the app counts the obligations a change hides and reveals under the FP-01 rule.

Markets (states 17 to 19; FP-04, AC-FP2, ADR 0026). A market is a country the platform covers, and its level is computed: operating when its jurisdiction term is in the scope, otherwise watching when a watch row exists, otherwise not followed. So the Jurisdiction group, now that the dimension mirrors the jurisdiction rows, is the markets we operate in, and it says so under its heading; it is an ordinary group, last because its dimension sorts after the prototype's seven, and it is added and removed through the same request as every other term. The panel below it writes the other level directly: one row per active country, the name and then either "Operating" as meta text or a Watching toggle that saves at once, with an audit row and no second person, because watching hides nothing. The European Union is not a country, so it has no row; what its rules reach is the "Also included" line, rendered from each jurisdiction's parent, Norway included through the EEA Agreement. "Everywhere else" says that research sweeps every market whatever is chosen here. The control is the foundations Toggle as a pressed button, not a switch, because foundations.md specifies no switch. Watching is carried by the toggle's border (border-neutral-01, 4.15:1 light and 7.05:1 dark against the card) and by the text colour, never by the fill alone, which measures 1.14:1 and would fail WCAG 1.4.11, exactly as the current tab is carried in navigation.md; aria-pressed carries it for a screen reader, and the label stays "Watching", so the accessible name holds the visible one and the market's name after it. There is no new pill and no new tone. A waiting scope request blocks the operating markets, not these toggles. A repeated watch answers 409 and an unwatched market 404; both render as state 19, and the toggle never invents a level the server did not send. Order follows the seed: Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland. The keys a market carries never reach a path or a query string (D-30), so nothing here is linkable.
