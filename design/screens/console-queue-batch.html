Batch review card (/console/queue/[proposalId] when the proposal is a batch). The kind is a notice pill (Re-tag) and the status the queue's own (Waiting warning, Approved positive, Rejected information). Each row names its record with the instrument in brand and the computed "Added when approved" (or "Removed when approved") in warning, and shows the library tags before and after as brand pills. A row is decided on its own, Approve or Reject, and stays undoable until the batch is applied; Reject opens a reason picker read from the rejection reason vocabulary with an optional note, and no row is rejected without a reason. Approve the rest applies every undecided row with the decisions already made, behind a passkey; Reject all asks for one reason and changes nothing. The editor who asked for the batch sees the four-eyes notice and Withdraw instead of any decision. The result names how many changed and how many did not, and links the one audit entry that carries each row's outcome. Ask for a re-tag files the batch: the tag picker answers the vocabulary's near-duplicate check in place, the record filter states how many match, and nothing changes until a second editor decides. Rows stack at 375 px, so nothing scrolls sideways; the batch actions stay in reach at the bottom.
