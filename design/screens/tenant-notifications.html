Notifications card (COL-02, and the preferences on PATCH /me; chunk 10). Route /notifications, an account destination beside My passkeys and My sessions (the registry's ACCOUNT parent), reached from the account row's menu from 1024px and from the account group of the More sheet below it. Any member; no permission gate and no step-up. The page is the person's own inbox, so there is no department or team view.

The bell is not a new rail row and not a fifth dock item: it is a dot on the account row's icon (and on More below 1024px) while anything is unread, and a "Notifications" item first in the account menu with the unread count as a quiet muted number at the end of the row. The count and the dot come from GET /me's counts (unreadNotifications), which the shell already reads, so no second request per page; marking read refreshes GET /me. Nothing unread: no dot and no number. The accessible name of the account row and of More says the count.

A row: the kind's pill, the record's title as the link (the stored title the notification was written with: a library or record title, never a comment, a note or any other text a person typed), then one line of meta: why (a catalog phrase with a person's name, a date or a count at most), then the time in the tenant's timezone ("Today 08:00", "Yesterday 14:20", then the date). Unread: a 8px notice-blue dot before the row, the title at 600 and the row on the subtle fill; read: no dot (its space kept, so titles align), the title at 500 on the surface. Opening the link marks the row read. "Mark as read" is a ghost button on unread rows only; "Mark all as read" sits in the header on the right and is disabled while nothing is unread. A row that arrived for someone who is away names them: "For Anna Nilsson, who is away" (the notification's onBehalfOf).

Newest first, 20 a page, "Show more" loads the next page. A record the reader can no longer open still lists with the title it was stored with; following it lands on the shared Not found (states.html), because the link answers 404.

Settings (MembershipNotificationPrefs on GET and PATCH /me): one switch per kind a person may turn off, saved at once on change with no Save button, each change its own PATCH of that one key. The switch is 36 by 20px with a 6px radius and a 16px thumb at 4px, because the fully rounded shape is the pill's alone (foundations.md); on is the primary colour, off the control border. Escalations are shown as held with Green's read-only glyph (foundations.md), never as a greyed switch, because the tenant sets them on /admin/workflow and a person cannot mute them. A refused save puts the switch back and says so.

The tone follows the kind's slot in pills-and-labels.md: information for a neutral fact, warning for something waiting on the reader, negative for the overdue end of the severity scale, notice for what kind of change this is. Labels are catalog phrases; the API sends the kind key. Names, titles, dates and counts are sample data.
