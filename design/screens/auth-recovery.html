Lost your passkeys?

There is no reset link and no password to fall back on.

Ask an administrator in your organisation to re-issue your enrolment. You will get a new invitation by email, enter a code once, and create a new passkey.

If you are the only administrator, contact bleqq support; they will check who you are before doing the same.

Recovery card, reached from "Lost your passkeys?" on the sign-in screen. It is a panel state of /sign-in, not a route. The only action is the way back. No email field: the address is never asked for here, so nothing can be enumerated.
