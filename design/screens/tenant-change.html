What happened

FI's board decided on 15 September 2026 to amend three regulations in the securities area following changed EU rules. Institutions that receive research must set criteria for an annual assessment of its quality, usability and value, and take action if the research does not contribute to better investment decisions.

Case panels, part A. One work panel shows at a time in the left column, chosen by the case's category: Triage for Needs triage, Next step for Assigned, Impact assessment from Assessing, with Actions under it once the assessment is saved. The status pill sits in the header's workflow status slot as information and reads the tenant's sub-status label ("Waiting for legal"); urgency takes its row's kind ("Within 3 months" warning, "Act now" negative). Urgency, owner, dismissal and close reasons, effort and status are the tenant's lists, sent as keys. Triage and dismissal need cases.triage; Start assessment, No action, Add action and Remove need cases.work; saving the assessment and completing, reopening or editing an action need cases.contribute; answering "No" needs cases.work because it closes the case. A viewer without the permission sees the panel's facts and no control, so no click can answer 403. No action and "No" close the case with one person's name on it and a reason from the close reason list; both can be moved back to triage, which part B draws with the closed state. The assessment and each action carry a version: a save sends If-Match, a 409 stale_write shows who saved and a reload, keeps the unsaved text on screen and never merges. While the case waits for sign-off, actions are locked and the assessment is read-only. An action is removed, never deleted: it leaves the list and stays in the case file. An open action past its due date shows "Overdue" as negative, computed from the tenant-local date; days left and days late are text in tabular numerals. Send as tickets is chunk 13 and is not on this card. Refusals render where the action was taken, from the server's answer.
