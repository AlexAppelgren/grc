FI adopts amended rules on paying for investment research

FinansinspektionenPublished 15 Sep 2026Source: fi.se, press release1 duplicate mergedFound by Research agent 0.4, 16 Sep 2026

What happened

FI's board decided on 15 September 2026 to amend three regulations in the securities area following changed EU rules. Institutions that receive research must set criteria for an annual assessment of its quality, usability and value, and take action if the research does not contribute to better investment decisions.
