Change facts card (the queue's Change facts filter, then one change). A library editor holding proposals review is the only person who reaches it; everyone else gets the denied state, and an organisation that disagrees with a confirmed fact uses "This looks wrong" on its own change screen. The screen settles the library's copy of a change and nothing else: an organisation's triage, its urgency, its "So what?" and its case links stay on tenant-change.html and are untouched by anything here. Tone comes from the slot or the kind, never from a person: change type is notice, urgency takes its kind's ordinal (Act now negative, Within 3 months warning, 6+ months notice, Monitor information, No action positive), flags and scope terms and the agent version are brand, the suggestion and match pills are information, a confirmation is positive, and a fact still waiting is warning. Slot order on the row and the header is change type, urgency, flags, then the count, with the authority and the date as plain meta text. Every term is picked from its vocabulary, so the screen stores a key and shows the label in the reader's language; an agent that submits a key the list does not hold is refused and nothing is stored, which is why state 7 shows a change with no type rather than a wrong one. Every timeline date carries the precision it was given, so "Q1 2027" and "Date not set" are real states and not missing data. Confirming needs no passkey, because it is not an approval and changes no permission; it does write the change, the audit entry and the re-index in one transaction, and the version the reader had open is checked first, which is state 11. The agent is named as its definition names it, Watch sweeper v1 (agents/watch-sweeper/v1); cards drawn before that definition landed say "Research agent 0.4" and are corrected when their chunk is built. Titles, confidences, counts and dates are sample data.
