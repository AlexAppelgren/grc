Enrolment card, steps two and three. The first passkey activates the account and swaps the enrolment session for a full one; the second-passkey prompt is the same registration ceremony again and may be skipped. "Skip for now" and "Add another passkey" share one row on phones, primary on the right. After the re-issue of an enrolment by an admin the same three steps run again. Step two asks for no name (Alex, 2026-09-19): the server names the passkey from the device, in this order: the authenticator's own name from its AAGUID ("Windows Hello", "Google Password Manager", "1Password"), else the browser and platform ("Chrome on Windows", "Safari on iPhone"), else the transport ("Security key", "Phone", "Passkey"), with a counter when the person already holds that name ("Windows Hello (2)"). Step three shows that name; renaming lives under My passkeys.
