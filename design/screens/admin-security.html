Security card (/admin/security), under Admin for security manage. One form, one Save, one passkey step-up for both panels. Passkeys we accept is a choice of two: any passkey, or device-bound passkeys of a model the admin ticks on bleqq's committed list. Tightening binds new registrations the moment it is saved and existing passkeys from the notice date, which defaults to 14 days ahead and is the admin's to move; loosening takes effect at once, so the date field disappears. A save that would leave the acting admin with no passkey that meets the policy is refused with 409 and drawn as its own panel with two ways forward, My passkeys and back to the list, never as a red banner, and nothing is saved. The session limits show the platform maximum beside each field, read from the platform's settings; above it the server's 422 renders under the field. No pill is added: every state is a sentence. Below the form, outside Save, "Our register and our own agents" is the tenant reach switch (ACC-08): off by default; one member holding security manage asks with a passkey, and the panel then names who asked and when in a warning banner; the asker sees no action, since the request's decider is never its requester, and another member holding security manage sees Reject and Approve with passkey. A 409 four_eyes_violation, if the asker's approval reaches the server, is drawn as its own panel, not as an error banner. On, the panel names who asked and who approved and how many entries read the register, and Switch off confirms once, asks for a passkey and takes effect at once for every entry, whatever each entry's own toggle says. The IP allow-list, single sign-on (chunk 13) and retention (chunk 12) arrive with their own cards. Two buttons per row on a phone, the primary on the right.
