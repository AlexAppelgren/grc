A unit's history opens from its row on the Units tab.

Units card. Reached from the Units panel of a standard's conformance obligation, and from the inventory filtered by a standard and a legal entity (the Statement of Applicability tab). The entity picker offers only entities whose conformance row applies; an entity that does not follow the standard is named in the hint, never offered. Add, Rename, Remove and Paste need register edit; Rename and Remove send If-Match and vanish once the unit has history, replaced by the fixed line. Setting applicability needs applicability approve and a confirmation dialog, never a second person or a passkey; a paste that carries decisions is one dialog listing them all and one call, capped by REGISTER_BULK_MAX, above which nothing is stored. The dry run stores nothing and names each refused line and why: a duplicate reference, an over-long title, an unknown decision. The form has no field for the standard's text. The Statement of Applicability shows each unit's reference, title, applicability, reason, status, who set it and when, under the conformance row's own assessed status, which no unit changes. Today counts the standard once. Every string goes to the register catalogs in en and sv.
