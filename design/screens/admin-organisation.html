Organisation card (TEN-01; departments and teams TEN-02 and TEN-03, chunk 8). Ask and AI drafts is the bank's own switch over its AI features (D-07, SRC-03): one button whose words say what it will do, the current state in a sentence naming the organisation, no pill. Switching is a security change: it needs security manage and a passkey step-up, the prompt opens from the button, and a cancelled prompt or a refusal renders in place with nothing changed. It switches this organisation's Ask and drafts only; the profile's Save never touches it. Any member may read the profile; saving needs security manage and the server's 403 renders in place with its detail and the missing grant. Languages come from the language rows as key and label; the timezone is a free IANA name with Nordic suggestions. The checklist is computed by the server; each undone step links to where it is done. Departments and teams need vocab.manage, as the legal entities do; they need no step-up and no second person, because neither grants access. A department is an org unit whose kind is business area, business unit or function, shown as plain meta text and not as a pill because this card adds no pill slot; it may sit under another, and its head is a member of this tenant. Naming a head is what puts the department on that person's scope switch on My work; the view itself is a filter any member may open. Who is in a team is set on the member row under Members, because that is members.manage. Retiring a team that still owns open work is refused, and merging two teams re-points the owner-team and participant rows.
