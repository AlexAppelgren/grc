Support access card, platform side (/console/support-access), in the console rail under the platform admin's group beside Tenants. Asking needs support access grant and no passkey, because a request grants nothing; Enter needs the same permission and a passkey, and turns the console session into a support session for that one bank until the window ends. The duration list stops at SUPPORT_ACCESS_MAX_HOURS, and the server refuses anything longer with a 422 rendered on the field. The list shows only the signed-in person's own grants. A bank is named by its organisation name and a decision by its time: the approver, the decliner and the revoker are members of the bank and are never named here, where the bank's own panel names them. The state pills are the bank card's: "Waiting for approval" warning, Active notice, and Ended, Revoked, Declined and Lapsed information. Any address inside a bank without a live grant renders Not found, exactly as a member of another bank sees it. Names, tickets and times are sample data.
