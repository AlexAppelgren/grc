Agent access card (/admin/agents/access and /admin/agents/access/[entryId]), the Access tab of Agents, for agent access manage alone: without it the tab is not offered and its address is Restricted, and another organisation's entry is Not found, the same answer as a missing one. The tab's one line says these agents read and change nothing; the first tab's line says what ours do. An entry row carries its state (Active positive, Revoked information) and what it reads now (Reads our register notice, Library only information): the pill is the effective state, so with tenant reach off every entry reads Library only. Departments and products are brand pills, the scope facets; naming none reads "All of our regulatory scope" as plain text, because empty means no narrowing. A credential carries its kind (Service, Personal: information) and Expired (warning) or Revoked (information) from its facts. Every write asks for a passkey: register, edit (If-Match, a stale version renders the 409 in place), revoke, the reach toggle, issuing a key and revoking one. A key names what it may read from the four read scopes and an expiry of at most 90 days, and its plain value shows once. The reach toggle is the entry's half of the switch; while the organisation's half is off it is disabled with that reason and a link to Security, never a second organisation switch. The access log names the credential, the person for a personal token, the tool, the filter names, the record count, the scope and the time taken, and never what was asked or answered; below 768 px each row stacks as label and value. Revoking an entry stops every credential under it on the next call and deletes nothing. Two buttons per row on a phone, the primary on the right.
